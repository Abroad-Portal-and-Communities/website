Getting started when you plan to work abroad.

## Before you go

- Research visa and work-permit requirements for your target country
- Clarify employment type (local hire, contractor, remote for a foreign employer)
- Budget for relocation, insurance, and tax obligations in both countries
- Join APC sharing sessions and ask others who have made a similar move

## Checklist

1. **Legal** — Right to work, contract review, social security — see [Legal & documents (Europe)](/docs/working-abroad/eu/legal-and-documents/) for a detailed checklist
2. **Finance** — Salary currency, banking, emergency fund
3. **Logistics** — Housing, healthcare, phone, local registration
4. **Community** — Language basics, time zone overlap with your team
