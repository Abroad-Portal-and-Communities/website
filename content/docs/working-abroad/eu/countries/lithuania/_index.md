Working in Lithuania as a foreign national. For EU-wide contract and document basics, see [Legal & documents (Europe)](/docs/working-abroad/eu/legal-and-documents/).

{{< callout type="warning" >}}
Rules change. Confirm with [Migration Department](https://www.migracija.lt/en/) and official advisers for your situation.
{{< /callout >}}

## Common routes (overview)

| Route | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| EU Blue Card | Qualified employment meeting salary and qualification criteria |
| National work permit + residence | Employer often initiates; category depends on role and skill level |
| ICT permit | Intra-corporate transfer |
| EU free movement | For EU/EEA/Swiss citizens — declaration of residence |

## Local steps (after entry)

Typical order; Vilnius, Kaunas, and other municipalities may differ slightly:

1. **Residence permit** — Apply or collect card at the Migration Department (*Migracijos departamentas*) if required for your nationality
2. **Address declaration** — Register place of residence with the municipality
3. **Personal code** (*asmens kodas*) — Needed for employment, contracts, and many services
4. **Social insurance** — Registration via employer (*SODRA*)
5. **Bank account** — Local IBAN for salary payments
