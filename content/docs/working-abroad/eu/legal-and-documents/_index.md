EU-wide overview of legal steps and paperwork. **National rules differ** — use a [country guide](/docs/working-abroad/eu/countries/) for local visas, registration offices, and official links.

{{< callout type="warning" >}}
This is community guidance, not legal advice. Confirm requirements with official government sources or a qualified adviser for your situation.
{{< /callout >}}

## Before you move

1. **Right to work** — Employment visa, EU Blue Card, ICT permit, family reunification, or EU citizenship (each has different eligibility).
2. **Employer documents** — Signed contract or assignment letter, job description, proof the role could not be filled locally (where required).
3. **Personal documents** — Valid passport, diplomas (often apostilled or translated), criminal record certificate (if requested), marriage/birth certificates for dependents.

## Contract & employment (common in the EU)

| Topic | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Contract type | Permanent, fixed-term, or contractor / B2B |
| Salary & currency | Gross vs net, payment schedule, bonuses |
| Probation | Length and notice during probation |
| Notice period | For you and the employer |
| Benefits | Pension, health insurance, paid leave |
| Remote / hybrid | Work location clause and tax implications |

## After arrival (typical steps)

Steps and names differ by country; your [country page](/docs/working-abroad/eu/countries/) should list local order and links.

- Register address with local authorities
- Obtain a tax identification number
- Register for social security / health coverage
- Open a local bank account (often needed for salary)
- Exchange or renew residence permit if required

## Document checklist (template)

Keep originals and certified copies where officials ask for both.

- [ ] Passport (valid beyond your planned stay)
- [ ] Signed employment contract
- [ ] Visa / residence permit approval (if applicable)
- [ ] Health insurance proof
- [ ] Proof of accommodation
- [ ] Educational credentials (and translations if required)
- [ ] Passport photos per local specification
