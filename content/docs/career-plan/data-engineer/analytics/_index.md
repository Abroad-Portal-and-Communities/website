Turn raw data into trusted metrics and models for analysts and stakeholders.

## Typical responsibilities

- dbt projects, dimensional models, and documentation
- Semantic layers and self-serve BI
- Partner with analysts on definitions and tests

## Learning path

1. Advanced SQL and warehouse concepts
2. dbt or similar transformation framework
3. BI tools (Looker, Metabase, Tableau, etc.)
4. Communication with product and business teams
